About AIM Journey

AIM Journey is an Erasmus+ Youth Exchange project that brings together young people from Austria and Poland to explore the opportunities and challenges of Artificial Intelligence in today's digital world.

The project combines AI, migration, digital storytelling, intercultural learning and active citizenship, encouraging participants to develop digital competences while promoting ethical AI use, inclusion and European values.

Through workshops, interactive activities, cultural exchange, local visits and collaborative learning, participants strengthen their critical thinking, creativity and teamwork skills. They also gain practical experience in responsible technology use and create digital stories reflecting their perspectives and experiences.

AIM Journey aims to empower young people to become responsible digital citizens who contribute to a more inclusive, innovative and connected Europe.

Project Objectives

Empowering Young People Through Artificial Intelligence and Intercultural Learning. Our mission is to equip youth with the knowledge and skills needed to navigate an increasingly digital world.

Promote Responsible AI

Increase understanding of AI and encourage its ethical, safe, and responsible use in everyday life.

Strengthen Intercultural Dialogue

Bring together youth from different backgrounds to encourage mutual understanding and respect.

Develop Digital Competences

Improve digital literacy, storytelling, media literacy, and responsible use of digital tools.

Raise Awareness of Digital Security

Understand online safety, privacy protection, and secure digital practices.

Encourage Inclusion and Diversity

Promote equal opportunities and social inclusion by exploring migration experiences.

Improve Communication & Teamwork

Strengthen leadership and teamwork skills through collaborative workshops.

Support Active Citizenship

Inspire youth to become active community members through civic engagement.

Disseminate Project Results

Share outcomes and materials with wider communities to ensure long-term impact.

Our Vision

To empower young people to use Artificial Intelligence ethically, embrace cultural diversity, strengthen digital competences and become active European citizens who contribute to a more inclusive, innovative and sustainable future.

"Connecting People, Empowering Minds, Shaping the Future with AI."
