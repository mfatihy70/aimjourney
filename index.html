AIM Journey

Together for Tomorrow: Young Voices of Integration

An Erasmus+ KA152 Youth Exchange project uniting young minds from Austria and Poland. Together, we explore migration, identity, social inclusion, and Artificial Intelligence through the powerful lens of creative digital storytelling.

Project Highlights

AIM Journey isn't just an exchange—it's a transformative experience. By bridging technology with human stories, participants learn to leverage Artificial Intelligence to express deeply personal and cultural narratives. Through non-formal learning methodologies, we break down borders and build a shared European identity.

Project Objectives

Our core mission is to empower youth with future-proof skills while fostering a deep sense of intercultural solidarity. We aim to equip participants with both the technical know-how of tomorrow and the ethical grounding needed for today.

Project Results

The tangible outcomes of our journey reflect the hard work, creativity, and collaboration of all 24 participants. From digital exhibitions to certified competences, these results serve as a lasting footprint of our European exchange.

  • 40+ Instagram Posts showcasing daily insights
  • AI Photo Gallery visualizing personal identities
  • AI Video Stories & a collaborative Final Movie
  • Youthpass Certificates documenting key competences
  • Digital Identity Exhibition celebrating diversity
