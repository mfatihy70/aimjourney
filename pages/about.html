About AIM-Journey

Project Details

AIM-Journey stands for "AI and the Migration Journey: Digital Integration Stories of Young People"[cite: 1]. The project aims to empower young participants to actively shape and communicate their own migration-related narratives, strengthen their digital and media literacy, and promote intercultural dialogue[cite: 1].

Who is involved?

The project involves 24 young people aged 16-25, with 12 participants from Austria and 12 from Poland[cite: 1]. The activities include a preparatory phase, a five-day Youth Exchange in Poland, and structured follow-up activities[cite: 1].

Our Objectives

  • Strengthen social inclusion and a sense of belonging among young people with migrant backgrounds[cite: 1].
  • Enhance digital competences and ethical awareness regarding AI use[cite: 1].
  • Promote intercultural dialogue and reduce stereotypes through collaborative storytelling[cite: 1].
