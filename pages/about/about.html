About AIM Journey

An Erasmus+ youth exchange project fostering cultural understanding, sustainable travel, and personal growth across Europe.

Our Mission

[Insert Mission Text Here]

We aim to connect young people from diverse backgrounds through the shared experience of train travel. Our goal is to break down cultural barriers and promote environmental consciousness by choosing rail over air travel.

Methodology
