Our 5-Day Adventure

A thrilling, immersive journey of non-formal learning, digital creativity, and vibrant cultural exchange in the heart of Warsaw.

Day 1

Arrival & Breaking the Ice

  • Touchdown in Warsaw: Participants arrive and settle into our creative hub.
  • Welcome Breakfast: A warm Austrian-Polish morning gathering.
  • Setting the Stage: Visionary welcome speech and project roadmap.
  • "Knowing Each Other": High-energy interactive ice-breakers and team building.
  • Twilight City Stroll: First magical glimpses of the Polish capital.
Day 2

Digital Identity & AI Innovations

  • Morning Briefing: Unlocking the potential of digital storytelling.
  • Masterclass Workshop: Designing personal AI Digital Avatars & crafting their core narratives.
  • Expert Keynote: A fascinating seminar on the future of science and AI by our esteemed Chemical Engineering Professor.
  • Evening Exploration: Free time to bond and explore Warsaw's local culture.
Day 3

Visual Storytelling & Deep History

  • Creative Kickoff: Transforming avatars into dynamic motion.
  • Action Workshop: Directing and generating compelling AI Video Stories.
  • Journey through Time: An emotional and educational visit to the Dialog Center.
  • Old Town Wonders: Guided immersive tour through Warsaw's historic, cobblestoned heart.
Day 4

Ethics, Community & Celebration

  • Digital Privacy Dive: Crucial presentations on AI Awareness and ethical tech boundaries.
  • The Ultimate Challenge: A fiercely fun and interactive Kahoot Quiz tournament.
  • Visionary Seminar: A deep dive into Ethics and forward-thinking leadership.
  • Retail & Culture: Exploring modern Warsaw and local shopping centers.
  • Grand Finale: Cheering together in a unified viewing of the World Cup Final!
Day 5

Reflections & Future Horizons

  • Honoring Resilience: A moving morning visit to the Warsaw Uprising Museum.
  • Crowning Achievements: Emotional closing speeches and the Youthpass Certificate Awarding Ceremony.
  • Digital Legacy: Strategy session for launching our joint Instagram awareness campaign.
  • Royal Promenade: A peaceful, reflective walk through the stunning Royal Park.
  • Farewell Gala: A final celebratory dinner filled with memories, laughter, and promises to reunite.
